## 🎯 **Learning Progression**

### **Beginner → Intermediate**

**When to Progress:**
- Mastered basic compound lifts
- Consistent training for 6+ months
- Ready for higher volume
- Understanding of proper form

**Transition Strategy:**
- From full-body → split routines
- From 3 days → 4-6 days
- From basic → advanced exercises
- From general → specific training

### **Intermediate → Advanced**

**When to Progress:**
- Training consistently for 18+ months
- Hit first plateau
- Need specialization
- Understanding periodization

**Transition Strategy:**
- From basic splits → advanced variations
- From linear → periodized progression
- From general volume → precise management
- From balanced → specialized training
